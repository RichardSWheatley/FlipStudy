FlipStudy: Flashcards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

FlipStudy is a flashcard study app. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. In short: your decks stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We do not have accounts and we do not collect analytics. The one service we run, FlipStudy Cloud, is used only by people we give a family code to.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. FlipStudy has no user accounts, no login, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. The decks, cards and study history you create are stored on your device using Apple's storage (SwiftData). We never receive them.

Syncing with iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, FlipStudy keeps your decks, cards and study streak in sync across your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit). That database belongs to your Apple Account: we cannot see or access it. Apple's privacy policy governs how iCloud stores it: apple.com/legal/privacy. Settings, such as your reminder time, stay on each device. If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on the device.

FlipStudy Cloud (family code only)

FlipStudy Cloud is an optional card-making service that works only after a family code we hand out is entered in Settings. Without a code, nothing in this section applies.

With a code turned on:

  • The text you scan or type to make cards, and any card you ask FlipStudy to explain, is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our service, which runs on Cloudflare and uses Cloudflare's AI models to write the reply. We do not store that text; it is used to answer the request and then discarded.
  • To keep codes from being shared beyond the family, our service stores a scrambled (hashed) form of the code, a random identifier for each device that used it, and a daily count of requests. None of these identify you.
  • You can stop at any time by choosing "Make cards on this phone instead" or removing the code in Settings.
  • Cloudflare processes these requests for us under its own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

On-device AI and translation (the default)

By default, FlipStudy generates cards and translations entirely on your device using Apple's built-in on-device models (Foundation Models and the Apple Translation framework). When you use these default features, no text leaves your device and nothing is sent to us or any third party.

Optional Cloud AI translation

FlipStudy includes an optional feature, turned off by default, that lets you use a third-party online translation service (Google Cloud Translation or Microsoft Translator) with your own API key that you enter in Settings.

If — and only if — you turn this on and provide your own key:

  • The topic text and study terms you ask to translate are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection directly from your device to the provider you chose (Google or Microsoft). The data is not routed through us.
  • Your API key is stored securely in the device Keychain and is used only to authenticate those requests. It is never sent to us.
  • The provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle that text:
    • Google: cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice
    • Microsoft: privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

You can stop using this feature at any time by turning off Cloud AI in Settings and removing your API key. When it is off, FlipStudy uses only on-device processing.

Camera and photo access

FlipStudy may ask for camera or photo library access so you can create cards from a page you photograph. Any text recognition happens on your device. Photos you choose are not uploaded or stored by us.

Children's privacy

FlipStudy is intended for students and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The default experience has no accounts and no data collection, and turning on FlipStudy Cloud or a cloud translator requires a grown-up to pass a check first. No personal information about a child is stored by us.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We do not collect personal information, so there is none to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
